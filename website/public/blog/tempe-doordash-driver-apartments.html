Common Mistakes Tempe DoorDash Drivers Make

Mistake #1: Apartment Hunting During Back-to-School

Why it fails: August-September is when 75,000 students flood Tempe looking for housing. Competition is insane, landlords are picky, and rents spike.

Fix: Sign your lease in May-July when students leave and landlords are desperate to fill units. You'll have more negotiating power, better rent rates, and easier income verification.

Mistake #2: Only Showing Summer Earnings

Why it fails: Summer is Tempe's slowest season with reduced student population. If you only show May-August income, it looks lower than your annual average.

Fix: Show a full year of earnings, or specifically highlight your peak fall/spring semester income to demonstrate your true earning potential.

Mistake #3: Ignoring Late-Night Hours

Why it fails: The 10pm-2am window on weekends is Tempe's most lucrative time, but many dashers skip it.

Fix: Even if you don't work late-night regularly, add a few Friday/Saturday night shifts. The earnings significantly boost your monthly totals and impress landlords.

Mistake #4: Living Too Far from Mill Avenue

Why it fails: If you live in Mesa or Chandler and commute to Tempe, you waste 20+ minutes each direction unpaid.

Fix: The modest rent savings from living far away are often offset by commute costs and lost earning time. South Tempe (85283) offers the best balance of affordable rent and quick access to campus zones.

Mistake #5: Not Documenting Game Day Earnings

Why it fails: ASU football and basketball games create massive earnings spikes that you might not capture in regular documentation.

Fix: Screenshot your earnings on game days. Include these in your income documentation to show landlords your surge earning potential.
